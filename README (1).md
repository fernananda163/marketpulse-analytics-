# 📊 MarketPulse Analytics

## E-commerce Marketing Performance Analysis

MarketPulse Analytics is a data analytics project that evaluates the
performance of four digital marketing channels throughout 2025:

-   Google Ads
-   Meta Ads
-   TikTok Ads
-   Email

The goal of the project is to transform raw marketing data into
actionable business insights using Python.

## 🎯 Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer:

-   Which marketing channel generates the most revenue?
-   Which channel delivers the highest return on advertising spend?
-   Which channel has the lowest customer acquisition cost?
-   How does marketing revenue change throughout the year?
-   Which channels may require optimization?

## 🛠️ Tools Used

-   Python
-   Pandas
-   NumPy
-   Matplotlib
-   Google Colab

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

-   CTR --- Click-Through Rate
-   Conversion Rate
-   CPC --- Cost Per Click
-   CPA --- Cost Per Acquisition
-   ROAS --- Return on Ad Spend

## 🔎 Key Insights

-   Email generated the highest total revenue: **\$915,342.24**
-   Email achieved the highest ROAS: **9.10**
-   Google Ads achieved the lowest CPA: **\$10.14**
-   Meta Ads had the highest ad spend but the lowest ROAS: **7.83**
-   May was the highest-revenue month: **\$339,638.21**
-   April was the lowest-revenue month: **\$236,564.58**

## 💡 Business Recommendation

Email and Google Ads demonstrated strong marketing efficiency during
2025 based on ROAS and CPA.

Meta Ads may benefit from additional campaign-level analysis to identify
opportunities to improve conversion efficiency and advertising returns.
